Division 02 — Lifestyle Medicine

Reverse the disease.
Not just manage it.

Every center is a fully integrated health hub — six clinical pillars, one location, one physician-led team.

Pillar 01

Prescription Nutrition

All dietary prescriptions fulfilled exclusively by EthioSavor Delights on-site. AI-generated plant-based meal plans prepared and delivered at the same center where you train and heal.

Pillar 02

Prescription Exercise

In-house commercial gym, group fitness classes, community jogging and walking groups — prescribed and monitored by ACSM-certified exercise physiologists.

Pillar 03 · Sleep Medicine

Comprehensive Sleep Medicine

Obstructive Sleep Apnea (OSA) — sleep study, CPAP, weight-loss pathway
Insomnia — CBT-I, first-line non-pharmacological treatment
Restless Leg Syndrome — evaluation & management
Circadian Rhythm Disorders — shift work, delayed/advanced sleep phase
Hypersomnia & Narcolepsy — specialist evaluation
Parasomnias — sleepwalking, night terrors, REM behavior disorder

Pillar 04

Stress Mastery

Dedicated yoga studio, mindfulness programs, biofeedback, and therapist-led group sessions — because chronic stress is a clinical condition requiring clinical intervention.

Pillar 05

Substance Avoidance

Tobacco cessation, alcohol reduction counseling, and pharmacotherapy — delivered with evidence-based precision and dignity.

Pillar 06

Social Connection

Physician-led weekly walking groups, peer support circles, and community health events — because loneliness is a chronic disease, and belonging is medicine.

AI-Powered Personalization

Every patient receives an AI-generated meal plan, exercise prescription, and sleep protocol — updated continuously based on biometrics, body composition, dietary habits, and behavioral data.
